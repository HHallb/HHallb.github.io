By combining tomography and digital volume correlation (DVC), we are able to capture the microscale adhesion mechanics in a multi-ply packaging material during peel testing. The details are found in a new paper, out now in [Tomography of Materials and Structures](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tmater.2026.100084).
