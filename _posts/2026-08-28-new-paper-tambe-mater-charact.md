New paper investigating how stitching local synchrotron X-ray tomography scans affects strain measurements by digital volume correlation. We find that stitching can increase strain uncertainty by a factor of 2-4 in overlapping regions, highlighting the importance of quantifying this uncertainty before analyzing _in-situ_ deformation experiments. Out now in [Materials Characterization](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matchar.2026.116852).
