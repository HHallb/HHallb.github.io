This paper shows how grain boundary inclination strongly affects energy anisotropy and shear-coupled motion in FCC materials, using systematic 3D phase field crystal (PFC) simulations of [001] and [111] tilt boundaries. Out now in [Acta Materialia](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actamat.2026.122379).
